I am a Computer Vision Scientist and Engineer at [RMIT](https://www.rmit.edu.au/), where I develop computer vision and AI-based solutions to address real-world industry challenges.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at the [Advanced Multimedia Research Lab](https://www.uow.edu.au/engineering-information-sciences/research/signals-information-and-communications-research-institute-sicom/), [University of Wollongong](https://www.uow.edu.au/), Australia, under the supervision of Prof. [Wanqing Li](https://scholars.uow.edu.au/wanqing-li).

My doctoral research focused on spatiotemporal representation learning for human motion analysis using 3D skeleton sequences, with applications in human action recognition and person identification. I have worked on projects involving Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs), Transformers, and Generative AI, including diffusion models for motion analysis.

Prior to my PhD, I worked as a Research Engineer at the [Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering](http://eng.pdn.ac.lk/deee/index.php) at the [University of Peradeniya](http://www.pdn.ac.lk/), Sri Lanka, where I also completed my B.Sc.Eng. (Hons) in Electrical and Electronic Engineering in 2019. My work there covered medical image analysis, low-light object detection, and control systems for robotic manipulators.
